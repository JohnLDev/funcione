# Privacy policy

Updated on 2026-07-24

## 1. Overview

This Privacy policy explains how Funcione, a MileX product, may collect, use, and protect personal data to provide access, registration, authentication, and training plan generation.

## 2. Data we may collect

We may process registration data, such as first name, last name, CPF, birth date, phone number, and email; authentication data; sport profile; information about availability, goals, restrictions, equipment, and plan history; plus technical records for security and operation.

## 3. Purposes of use

We use data to authenticate users, maintain registration, personalize training requests, generate plans with artificial intelligence support, improve the experience, display advertising through Google AdSense, prevent abuse, fix issues, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

## 4. Sharing and processors

We may use technology providers for hosting, database, authentication, observability, artificial intelligence processing, and advertising. Google AdSense may use advertising cookies, identifiers, and browsing signals to deliver, measure, and protect ads, according to the applicable consent and privacy settings.

## 5. User rights

You may request access, correction, update, deletion, or information about the processing of your data through MileX official channels, subject to applicable legal bases and retention obligations.

## 6. Security and retention

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect data. We keep information for as long as necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect rights.

## 7. Updates

This policy may be updated as the product evolves, new features are released, or legal requirements change.
